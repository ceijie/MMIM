About MMIM

MMIM is a Bible-believing Christian church committed to worship, discipleship, prayer, evangelism, missions, and serving communities through the love of Jesus Christ.



Our Purpose

We exist to REACH people for Jesus Christ by EQUIPPING the believers, develop their ABILITIES in serving the Lord and to CARE for one another in order to HONOR God's name.



Our Vision

To reach the whole Philippines, 10/40 window countries, and the world through intentional evangelism and missions which will result in planting 500 viable congregations by the year 2030.
